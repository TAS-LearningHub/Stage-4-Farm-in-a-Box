Farm in a Box: Grow More with Less

Situation: Useful agriculture can happen in a small space. The challenge is to create a compact crop-production system that makes careful use of water and other resources, can be monitored with simple records, and can be improved when conditions change.

Design brief: Design, establish, monitor, improve and evaluate a portable Farm in a Box crop-production system using the crop, location, materials, tools and processes approved by your teacher. Use evidence to show how the system supports plant growth, manages resources and could be improved for another growing cycle.

Your folio should show

  • a clearly defined need, intended users and measurable success criteria;
  • research into the selected crop and its growing requirements;
  • at least two genuinely different annotated design ideas;
  • a justified final design and production plan;
  • teacher-approved safe production and establishment;
  • a field record containing dates, observations, measurements and decisions;
  • at least one evidence-based test or modification; and
  • a final judgement against the criteria and a specific next-version proposal.

Success criteria

  • Function
    The system performs its intended crop-support function under the available conditions.
  • Resource use
    Choices avoid unnecessary water, material and crop waste and are justified with evidence.
  • Safe production
    The approved project uses the teacher’s current local controls.
  • Manageability
    The system can be monitored and maintained within the available time, place and care arrangements.
  • Communication
    Design and production decisions are clear and supported by relevant evidence.
  • Improvement
    Testing leads to a specific evidence-based improvement or next-version proposal.

Crop failure is usable evidence. Delayed germination, unexpected growth, weather or disrupted access can be recorded, diagnosed and used for redesign. Your teacher may provide a fallback image or data set so you can complete equivalent analysis.

The brief does not approve a crop, site, container, growing medium, tool, chemical, process or local safety control. Those choices must come from your teacher.

Backup versus readable export

JSON backup is the editable recovery file and includes optional small images. Readable text contains your written evidence and image captions, but not the image data.

Export is not submission

Printing or exporting preserves your work. It does not send anything to a teacher, verify safe practice, prove teacher observation or replace any school process your teacher later confirms.
