Before teaching

Teacher to confirm for this class

The course deliberately leaves these local decisions open. Record the current class decision or source below; retain approved school documents in the school’s authorised system.

Ready. Preparation notes save after each change.

Delivery position and sequence

Confirm the class, delivery year, rotation position and whether this unit replaces or sits beside another food-and-agriculture unit.

Growing site and care

Confirm light, water access, storage, security, weekend and holiday care, plus an accessible observation pathway.

Crop and fallback evidence

Confirm approved crop options and a fallback image/data set for delayed germination, crop failure or disrupted access.

Materials, tools and budget

Confirm containers, growing media, water-delivery components, permitted tools, available quantities and budget. Sensors remain optional.

Local practical controls

Confirm current demonstrations, supervision, PPE, warnings, labels, relevant SDS/SOP and school risk controls before practical work.

Produce pathway

Confirm whether produce is only measured and photographed. Any consumption requires separate current food-safety and allergy controls.

Cultural source and local engagement

Use only named public material unless local Community or AECG involvement and permission are confirmed. Do not represent non-local cases as local Cultural Knowledge.

Grouping, access and evidence

Confirm roles, accessibility adjustments, device access, photograph alternatives and which evidence is expected from each student.

Formal assessment status

Confirm whether the unit contributes to a formal task. Add marks, weighting, dates, notification, criteria and submission rules only from current school and faculty authority.
